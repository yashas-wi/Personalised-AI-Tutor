# Computer Vision and Convolutional Neural Networks

## Fundamentals of Image Processing in AI
Images are structured as multidimensional arrays (tensors) of pixel intensities. A standard color image has dimensions (Height, Width, Color Channels).

## Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)
CNNs leverage spatial locality and translation invariance through three core operations:
1. Convolution Layer: Filters/Kernels slide across feature maps performing element-wise multiplication and summation.
2. Pooling Layers: Max Pooling or Average Pooling reduces spatial dimensions.
3. Activation (ReLU/GELU): Introduces non-linearity following convolution operations.

## Vision Transformers (ViT)
Modern computer vision increasingly applies transformer architectures directly to images by tokenizing non-overlapping patches and processing them with standard Transformer encoder blocks.
